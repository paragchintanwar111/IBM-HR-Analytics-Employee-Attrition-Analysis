# IBM HR Analytics – Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to understand attrition patterns and identify factors associated with employees leaving the organization. Python was used for data cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), and visualization.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall employee attrition.
* Identify departments and job roles with high attrition.
* Understand the relationship between overtime and attrition.
* Analyze attrition patterns by age, monthly income, and years at the company.
* Identify factors associated with employee turnover.

## Dataset

* **Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance
* **Records:** 1,470 employees
* **Features:** 35 columns
* **Target Variable:** Attrition

## Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

## Project Workflow

1. Loaded and inspected the dataset.
2. Checked missing values and duplicate records.
3. Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
4. Analyzed attrition across employee demographics and job roles.
5. Studied the relationship between overtime, income, age, and attrition.
6. Created visualizations to communicate key findings.

## Key Insights

* Overall employee attrition was approximately 16.12%.
* Employees working overtime had a higher attrition rate than employees not working overtime.
* Sales Representatives had a relatively high attrition rate.
* Employees who left the company had a lower average monthly income than employees who stayed.
* Younger employees showed higher attrition rates than older employees.

## Conclusion

This project helped identify employee groups with higher attrition rates and explore factors associated with employee turnover. The analysis can help HR teams better understand attrition patterns and support data-driven workforce planning.
